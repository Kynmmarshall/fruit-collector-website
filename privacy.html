Privacy Policy

Fruit Collector

This policy explains how the Fruit Collector website handles information when you visit the site, open the download page, or contact us for support.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account on this website. The site may collect basic usage data through analytics tools so we can understand page visits and improve the experience.

How we use it

Any information gathered is used to keep the website working properly, measure traffic, and respond to support requests sent by email.

Third-party services

This site uses an analytics service to view anonymous usage trends. If you contact us by email, your message will be handled through your mail provider.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the website, standard browser and device information may be processed by our analytics provider, including page views, approximate location, browser type, device type, and referral data. We use this data in aggregate form and do not use it to identify you personally.

Information you choose to share

If you contact us for support, you may share your name, email address, and any details included in your message. We use that information only to respond to your request and maintain support records.

Data retention

We keep support messages only as long as needed to handle the request and maintain basic records. Analytics data is stored according to the retention settings of the service provider.

Your choices

  • You can avoid analytics tracking by using browser settings, privacy tools, or tracker blocking extensions.
  • You can contact us to ask about any information you previously shared by email.
  • You can stop visiting the site at any time without creating an account or signing in.

Children's privacy

Fruit Collector is a game website, but the site itself is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.
